Print design, for an assignment.

Atas Angin's a real brand, they run live houses and shows for indie and local acts. The assignment didn't require contacting them, just picking a brand and designing a brochure around it, so this was really an exercise in figuring out what kind of graphic design suits me. Went grunge the whole way through. Took two to three weeks, and it's one of the more fun assignments I've had for exactly that reason, it was less about the brief and more about finding my own style inside it.
