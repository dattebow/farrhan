Thank you for choosing me for your VOD scrubbing needs. Please take a moment to read through these terms carefully.

**Services provided**<br>
A full watch-through of your provided VOD. Delivery of a timestamped document listing clip titles, moment descriptions, and my top picks flagged. Providing up to <mark>2 rounds of revisions</mark> if you feel I missed something significant. Additional revision rounds may incur extra charges.

**Requirements**<br>
Provide the VOD as a Twitch link, YouTube link, or MP4 file. For Twitch clip creation (add-on), Editor access or "Everyone can clip" must be enabled while I work. For MP4 exports (add-on), files are delivered via a shared Google Drive folder.

**Payment terms**<br>
<mark>A non-refundable deposit of RM30 is required before work begins.</mark> The remaining balance is due within 7 days of delivery. There will be <mark>no cancellation fee</mark> if the project is cancelled before work begins.

Final pricing is confirmed once I review the VOD length and any specific requirements.
